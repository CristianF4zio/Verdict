Both MailForge and SendWave target the same audience: creators and small teams who've outgrown a basic newsletter tool. Here's how they actually compare.

## Automation

MailForge's builder supports deeper branching logic. SendWave trades some of that flexibility for a faster setup experience.

## Deliverability

Both platforms performed comparably in our inbox placement tests, with SendWave holding a slight edge on Gmail specifically.

## Pricing

SendWave undercuts MailForge at the low end, but MailForge's curve is flatter as your list grows.

## Which should you pick?

Choose MailForge if you rely on complex automation. Choose SendWave if you want to be sending campaigns within the hour.
