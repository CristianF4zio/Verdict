We tested nine email marketing platforms over three months. These three came out on top for creators specifically, not marketing teams at bigger companies.

## 1. MailForge: best for automation

Best if you want deep branching logic without writing custom code.

## 2. SendWave: best for getting started fast

Best if you want to send your first campaign the same day you sign up.

## 3. DripCraft: best for paid newsletters

Best if monetizing your list directly, without a separate billing tool, matters most.

## How we tested

We sent the same campaign sequence through each platform to a seeded list and tracked inbox placement, setup time, and total cost at 1,000 and 10,000 subscribers.
