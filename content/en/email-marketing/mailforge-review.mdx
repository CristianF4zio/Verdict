MailForge has spent the last two years building out its automation engine, and it shows. We tested it against a real newsletter list of a few thousand subscribers to see how it holds up day to day.

## How we tested MailForge

We ran MailForge for 45 days against a live list of 1,200 subscribers, migrating automation sequences from another platform to measure real migration friction.

## Deliverability

Inbox placement was consistently strong during our testing window, with only minor dips during high-volume sends.

## Automation builder

The visual automation builder is where MailForge stands out: branching logic and conditional splits are easy to set up without leaving the canvas.

## Pricing

Plans scale with subscriber count rather than send volume, which is friendlier for creators who send often. The Creator plan starts at $9/mo for up to 300 subscribers; visual automation only unlocks on Creator Pro, at $29/mo.

## Verdict

For creators who want strong automation without an enterprise price tag, MailForge is a solid pick.
