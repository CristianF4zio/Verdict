MailForge has spent the last two years building out its automation engine — branching logic, conditional splits, and a migration tool that made switching from our old platform nearly frictionless. We ran it for 45 days against a live list of 1,200 subscribers to see how it holds up beyond the demo.

## What stood out

The visual automation builder handles complex branching without touching code, and inbox placement stayed strong even during high-volume sends.

## Pricing

Plans scale with subscriber count rather than send volume, which is friendlier for creators who send often. The Creator plan starts at $9/mo for up to 300 subscribers; visual automation unlocks on Creator Pro, at $29/mo.
